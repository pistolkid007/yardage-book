# Yardage Book changelog

Version numbers: MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. Minor = new features or new courses; patch = fixes and data corrections.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-03
- Version number shown at the bottom of the page.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-03
- First shared release: 34 courses with scorecards; 24 mapped from OpenStreetMap (fairway widths, bunkers, water, houses, hole directions).
- Hole-by-hole Preferred / Aggressive / Safe plays from your own bag (set it in Settings).
- Play mode: shot calculator with wind, slope and lie; stroke and putt counter; scorecard; hole notes.
- Everything you enter stays in your own browser.
